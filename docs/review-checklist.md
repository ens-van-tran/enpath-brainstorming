# Review Checklist

Use this list during product and UX review.

## Organization

- Team creation is discoverable and uses no Department terminology.
- Team supports enabled reusable Roles, one Primary Manager, and Members with valid Role + Level.
- Missing Manager, Role, Member, Framework, or Career Path produces an explicit blocker.

## Framework and Career

- Library, Template, Team Framework, Published Revision, and Assessment Snapshot boundaries are understandable.
- Manager edits remain Team Framework-local.
- Career Path switches by Team and the Employee tree remains readable on desktop and mobile.
- Target Position never looks like a promotion or vacancy workflow.

## Assessment

- Employee Self-Assessment is distinct from Manager Assessment.
- Manager Assessment remains locked until Self-Assessment submission.
- `Unknown`, `Not Answered`, `Not Configured`, and `Not Comparable` are not conflated.
- Assigned scope is described as immutable.

## Development and Reassessment

- Plan ownership remains with the Employee.
- `Reviewed` is advisory and snapshot-specific, not approval.
- Completed Actions can be linked as reusable Evidence.
- Multi-Competency requests show independent item decisions.
- Contextual Reviewer cannot rate or inspect unrelated content.

## Capability

- Achieved comparable capability and development coverage are visually separate.
- Aggregate views do not expose private Plan, Evidence, Self-Assessment, or raw reviewer content.
