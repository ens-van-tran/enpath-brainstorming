# En-Path Product Documentation

En-Path is a configurable competency development and assessment framework platform. Organizations configure Teams, reusable Roles, Competencies, Frameworks, Career Paths, and expectations while the product keeps rating, authority, evidence, assessment, and history semantics stable.

## Current review surface

The clickable prototype now covers four Product Roles:

- **HR:** Team setup, Members, Roles and Levels, Competency Library, Templates, Team Frameworks, Team Career Paths, Capability Priorities, and governance.
- **Manager:** Team Framework configuration, Learning Resources, Assessments, Official Ratings, advisory Plan Review, and Reassessment decisions.
- **Employee:** competency profile, visual Team Career Path, Self-Assessment, Development Plans, Evidence, Initial Assessment, and multi-Competency Reassessment.
- **Contextual Reviewer:** invitation-scoped qualitative review without rating authority.

[Open the local prototype guide](prototype.md){ .btn }

## Fixed product semantics

1. The system uses exactly five Rating Levels.
2. `Unknown` is not Level 1 and has no numeric value.
3. Self-Assessment and Official Rating remain separate.
4. Only the responsible Manager issues Official Ratings.
5. Employees own Development Plans, Actions, and Evidence.
6. Team is the organizational scope; En-Path has no separate Department domain.
7. Historical decisions render from preserved snapshots.
8. Career and development create no promotion, vacancy, staffing, or compensation commitment.
